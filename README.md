## 👨‍💻 About Me

<img align="right" width="360" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif" alt="Developer at work" />

- 👋 Hey there, I'm **Waqas Khan**, a Frontend Engineer who loves turning designs into smooth, fast UIs.
- 💼 I build product UIs with **Next.js, React and TypeScript**, plus **React Native** for mobile.
- 🚀 Most recently at **tracebloc** (Jun 2025 – Nov 2026), a platform for testing and training AI models on private data. I shipped **~970 merged PRs** there across the product app, the website and the design system.
- 🧩 Moved a large product app off MUI onto an in-house design system, with custom ESLint rules and CI guards to keep it clean.
- ⚡ Made sites faster, safer and easier to find: Core Web Vitals, strict CSP, SEO and structured data.
- 🧪 Testing with Vitest, Cypress and mutation testing, because "it works on my machine" isn't a strategy.
- 🤖 AI-assisted dev every day with Claude Code, plus custom skills and workflows for it.
- 👯 **Open to new frontend / full-stack roles and collaborations!**
- 📫 Reach me at: **waqasthewebmaster@gmail.com**
- 🏡 Based in the beautiful city of Mardan, KP, Pakistan 🇵🇰
